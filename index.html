We are looking for native speakers to read short sentences aloud and record them on a mobile phone. The recordings are used to train speech recognition (ASR) models. Individuals and vendors can apply.

You must be a native speaker of the language you apply for. The number shows how many sentences each participant reads.

  • Adults (18+) — balanced male and female speakers
  • Smartphone (Android or iPhone)
  • A normal quiet room for recording
  • Clear reading of 150–900 sentences depending on the language (about 1 hour of valid audio)
